The everyday challenge

Are your landscaping books making it hard to know if you’re actually profitable?

Running a landscaping business means managing more than lawns, gardens, and projects. You're handling customer payments, payroll, materials, equipment, fuel, and daily expenses. When the records aren't organized, it's hard to see what's happening behind the work.

  • Disorganized records. Receipts, invoices, bank transactions, and expenses are scattered across files and systems.
  • Unpaid invoices. You aren't sure who still owes you, how long balances have been outstanding, or what needs follow-up.
  • Unclear job profitability. Your estimates look reasonable, but you don't know if actual projects made the profit you expected.
  • Hidden project costs. Labor, materials, fuel, equipment, and subcontractors may be costing more than planned.
  • No clear picture. You may not know which services, crews, or types of jobs are contributing most to your business.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

What types of landscaping businesses do you help?

I work with landscaping and outdoor-service contractors, including lawn care and maintenance businesses, landscape installation and hardscape contractors, irrigation specialists, tree care businesses, and other outdoor-service providers.

Do you work with QuickBooks?

I can work with QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Desktop, depending on your business setup and the scope of the engagement. I can also work with spreadsheets and other existing records as needed.

My books are behind or messy. Can you help?

Catch-up bookkeeping and financial record cleanup are areas of support. We'll first assess the condition of your records, identify what needs organizing or reconciling, and determine a practical approach.

Can you help me see unpaid customer invoices?

Yes. I can organize and monitor accounts receivable, review aging balances, identify outstanding invoices, and help establish a consistent process for tracking and follow-up.

Can you help me understand whether my jobs are profitable?

Yes. By organizing job-related expenses and comparing estimated costs against actual results, we can develop a clearer view of project performance and identify areas where estimates, expenses, or pricing may need attention.

What is job costing?

Job costing tracks expenses associated with a specific project. For landscaping contractors, this may include labor, materials, equipment, subcontractors, and other direct costs. It helps compare what you expected to spend with what you actually spent.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the condition of your books, transaction volume, services needed, and complexity of your operations. After our initial check-in, I can recommend a scope of work and provide a pricing proposal.

Is my financial information confidential?

Your business financial information is treated as confidential and handled with care. Information is used for purposes related to agreed services and communication, subject to applicable engagement terms and privacy practices.
